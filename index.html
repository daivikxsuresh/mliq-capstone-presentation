04 Project Goals

Turn every empty date into a priced decision.

The Glasshouses — what they're optimizing for
01
Fill the calendar — maximize booked days across all four venues, especially during peak seasons
02
Deliver remarkable experiences — maintain the prestige of NYC's preeminent private event space across 75,000 sq ft
03
Win the right events — attract galas, weddings, product launches and marquee clients that match venue scale & brand
04
Remove pricing guesswork — replace judgment calls with a defensible, data-backed quote for every incoming lead
01
Give the sales team a defensible price benchmark for every incoming lead — replacing case-by-case judgment.
02
Quote each lead by the opportunity cost of committing the date, netted against the fixed cost of an empty day.
03
Recover the ~$8.9M of uncaptured in-season capacity that leaks out as unsold days.
04
Ship a production tool that runs on every quote and writes back to Salesforce — with human overrides.

05 Immediate Next Steps

We follow CRISP-DM, the standard cycle for data-mining projects. The first turn is business understanding — pinning down the decision and the success metric with The Glasshouses before any modeling begins.
